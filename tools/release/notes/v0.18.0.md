## Safer sign-in, vendors and purchasing, relays, phones, and every kind of printer

This release is the largest yet. **Signing in** gains throttling, two-factor sign-in (an authenticator app, recovery codes and passkeys) and **identity providers** over OpenID Connect, OAuth2 and SAML. BAM! now **sends email**, posts **webhooks**, and fires **hooks on events and schedules**. It keeps **vendors, purchases, contracts and orders**, and assets gain **attachments, comments, review states and person fields**. **Relays** reach printers on other networks. A phone **installs BAM! from the instance** and works **offline**. An **assistant** sits beside the page. And BAM! prints to far more printers, shows labels on **e-paper displays**, and engraves them with **lasers**.

### Signing in
- **Password guessing is slowed.** After 5 wrong passwords in 15 minutes, an account waits before its next try, from 1 second doubling to 15 minutes. It is never locked. An address with 20 wrong passwords across accounts waits 15 minutes. An email with no account takes as long to answer as a real one, so timing no longer says which emails have accounts.
- **Two-factor sign-in.** Set up an authenticator app under Your account. **Recovery codes** sign in when the phone is lost, and a **passkey** can be the second factor. Whoever manages users removes someone's factors from the Users page, and `bam admin mfa reset` does it for anyone.
- **Required by role.** A role can require two-factor sign-in, the admin role's included (`bam admin mfa require`).
- **Identity providers.** Sign in through **OpenID Connect** (Google, Microsoft Entra, Authentik, Keycloak and others), plain **OAuth2** (such as GitHub) or **SAML 2.0**. Accounts can be made on first sign-in, with roles from the provider's groups. Providers are set under Admin, or in `config.toml` for instances set up by a script.
- **Account emails.** Invitations, password resets and second-factor recovery go out by email.

### Email, webhooks and hooks
- **Email.** Notifications go to any SMTP server, set under `[email]`. Borrowers can be **reminded of their loans** by email.
- **Webhooks** post events to any URL as signed JSON.
- **Every channel** shows its deliveries, and one that keeps failing is turned off.
- **Hooks fire on events and schedules,** not only from buttons. **Incoming hooks** take requests from outside BAM!, signed with a secret.
- **Upgrades are announced.** BAM! records when it is upgraded or rolled back, and, with `[updates] check` on, when a newer release is out.

### Vendors and purchasing
- **Vendors**, and how to reach them.
- **Where each asset came from:** its vendor, dates, price, warranty and invoice. Prices are shown only to roles with the new `purchases.read`.
- **What each vendor sells,** with part numbers and prices, and **contracts** with reminders before they end.
- **Purchase orders,** and receiving what comes into stock.
- **Pooled stock.** Stock kept in several places counts together, and a category can imply others.

### Assets
- **Attachments.** Assets and categories carry files: images, which is what photos were, and documents such as PDFs.
- **Comments** in Markdown on assets, categories, loans and filings, with **@mentions**.
- **Favorites.** Star assets, categories, reports and flows.
- **Person fields** name people, including people who have no account yet.
- **Review states.** Assets are draft, confirmed or verified, with dates to review them by.
- **Links** are followed through several hops and drawn. A flow can link the asset it finds or files to others, in a **Link** step.

### Import and export
- **Several files** are imported at once, each previewed and imported on its own.
- **Excel.** Import `.xlsx` workbooks a sheet at a time, and export assets and report tables to Excel.
- **PDF.** Export reports and asset lists, and print an asset's sheet or a stocktake checklist.

### Phones and the assistant
- **Install BAM! on a phone** from the instance itself: Chrome on Android offers **Install app**, and Safari on an iPhone offers **Share › Add to Home Screen**.
- **Offline.** The installed app keeps a copy of what you can see. With no signal, it still scans labels, finds things and keeps the shopping list.
- **The camera reads codes on iOS** too.
- **An assistant** beside the page answers questions and acts, through BAM!'s own tools and with your permissions. It needs `assistant.use`.

### Relays
- **`bam relay`** runs beside printers on another network and prints for a BAM! hosted elsewhere. It links over BoneMesh or WebSocket.
- **Pairing** uses a one-time code. Relays can be revoked and watched under Admin.

### Printing
- **New drivers:**
  - ZPL and EPL2 (Zebra and the many printers that speak ZPL);
  - the rest of Brother's QL range, the TD series and P-touch;
  - more TSPL printers;
  - DYMO LabelManager;
  - Epson LabelWorks, the LW-PX800 included;
  - Brady's M611, in its own format;
  - ESC/POS, Phomemo and Niimbot;
  - Datamax, SATO and Godex in their own languages.
- **Color.** Labels are drawn in a printer's inks, and Brother's QL-800 series prints red and black.
- **Bluetooth.** Print to Bluetooth LE printers from the server or a relay, or **from the browser**, over the browser's own Bluetooth.
- **The Windows spooler connection** has now been run on Windows.

### E-paper displays
- **Displays are printers.** Show labels on e-paper displays, pushed to them or pulled by them.
- **Supported:** OpenEPaperLink tags, TRMNL, any display that fetches an image (such as an ESPHome build), webhooks, paperlesspaper's OpenPaper frames, and SoluM shelf labels through AIMS.

### Lasers
- **One-shot printers.** Engrave and cut a template onto a workpiece, on xTool lasers, GRBL lasers and Ruida controllers.
- **Camera alignment.** On a laser with a camera, the label is placed wherever the workpiece sits on the bed.
- **Running a job** needs the new `lasers.run` permission.

### Fixed
- **Signing in** answered at once for an email with no account, so its timing showed which emails had accounts.
- **Archives from before a migration that added a table** failed their check on restore.
- **A flow's `/f/` address** wrapped onto two lines in Chromium.

### Upgrading
- **Back up first,** as always. Migrations 051 to 084 run on start. Backups from earlier versions restore and migrate forward.
- **Set `[auth] secret_key`** (or `BAM_AUTH_SECRET_KEY`) if your configuration has none: 32 random bytes as base64, which `openssl rand -base64 32` makes. `bam init` writes one for new instances.
  - **What needs it:** second factors, identity providers, relays, incoming hooks, and the paperlesspaper and SoluM accounts are all sealed with it.
  - **Keep it the same once set:** with another key, what was sealed with the old one can't be read.
- **Behind a reverse proxy, set `server.trusted_proxies`** (`BAM_SERVER_TRUSTED_PROXIES`) to the proxy's addresses. Without it, every request seems to come from the proxy. Sign-in throttling would then count every wrong password, from anyone, against that one address.
- **Photos are now attachments.** Migration 067 moves them, keeping their IDs and files. The photo routes still answer as before.
- **New permissions:**
  - `comments.write` and `comments.moderate` go to every role that writes or deletes assets;
  - `vendors.manage` goes to every role that manages categories;
  - `purchases.read` goes to every role that reads assets, where it reads them;
  - `assets.confirm` goes to every role that edits assets;
  - `people.add` goes to every role with `users.manage`;
  - `assets.verify`, `assistant.use` and `lasers.run` go to the admin role only.

  Check that your roles hold what you want, and take `purchases.read` away from roles that shouldn't see prices.
- **Incoming hooks are saved only from a signed-in session.** An API or personal token can't make or change one, and MCP has no tool for it.
- **New configuration tables, each off or defaulted until set:**
  - `[email]` and `[updates]`;
  - `[notifications]` keep and turn-off settings;
  - `[relay]` and `[printers] bluetooth`;
  - `[attachments]`, `[purchases]` and `[contracts]`.

  [Configuration](https://github.com/Cambridge-Hackspace/boring-asset-manager/blob/main/docs/configuration.md) lists them.
- **The README's detail** has moved into `docs/`, a page for each part.
