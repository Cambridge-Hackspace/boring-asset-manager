## A new look, and every screen reworked

This release gives bam **a look of its own**, in light and dark: warm paper and ink, a red primary button with a hard shadow, and Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, served from bam itself. **Every screen is reworked** to go with it, so each has one primary action, controls big enough to tap, and nothing that scrolls sideways on a phone. A flow's run now says **how far along it is**, step 2 of 3. And the README and help are rewritten as prose.

### The look
- **Colors:** the grays are redrawn warm, from paper to ink, with the contrast between any two shades as it was. Text meets 4.5:1 against what it sits on, and borders and focus rings 3:1, in both schemes; a test checks every pair.
- **Controls:** buttons, fields and cards have 2 px borders and are 44 px tall. The template editor's dense controls are 32 px.
- **Dark:** the same design on near-black.
- **Fonts** are served from bam, with their OFL licenses, so the content security policy still allows fonts from bam alone.

### Finding your way
- **The header** links to Catalog, Labels and Admin, each opening its first page. The pages of the section you are in sit in a row beneath it, with the current one marked. The drop-down menus are gone; phones keep the Menu button.
- **Every page** has the same header: its title, a line about what it is for, and its actions.
- **Home** leads with looking something up. Beneath it are cards for what is running low, what needs attention and what is due back this week, then every page by section.

### The everyday screens
- **An asset's page** has two columns: its photos, details, links, contents and history, and beside them cards for where it is, stock, its loan, labels, actions and flows. Its codes sit under its name.
- **Scan** puts the camera first. A scanned asset's quick action is its one primary button.
- **Loans** filter with a segmented control, and **Flows** are cards to run, edit or print as a poster.
- **The print queue** switches between Waiting, Jobs and Printed with a segmented control.
- **Users** can be found by name or email. **Your account** has two columns.

### The catalog and editors
- **A category's page** is in tabs: About, Fields, Stock and lending, Labels, Icon, Links and actions, and Who can see it. Each tab appears only when it has something for you. Changes not yet saved in one tab survive looking at another, and that tab says so. The tab is kept in the address, so a link can open it.
- **The icons page** is in tabs too: every icon, the library, the palette, asset states and settings.
- **Choosing categories** for a flow or a report uses a picker that opens one level at a time, with search across every level and the arrow keys.
- **The flow editor** lists its steps in order and opens one at a time. The preview beside it shows that step.
- **The template editor's panels** say what they hold beside their titles, open or closed: the label's size, how many layers, what is selected, how many changes, and the grid.

### Flows
- **Step 2 of 3:** a run shows how far along it is above each question, with a bar of one segment for each step. Only steps that ask something count. A choice that brings in a step shown only for some answers raises the total.
- **The run** shows the asset it is about above each question.
- **The editor's preview** counts the steps the same way.
- **The API and MCP:** a run's view gains `progress`, with `step` and `of`, and the MCP run tool's summary says "2 of 3".

### The writing
- **The README** is prose where it was bullets, with lists kept for what really is a list, and subheadings where a topic is large.
- **Help** says where the moved controls are now.

### Fixed
- **The icons page** scrolled sideways on a phone, and its fields, file input and gallery links were too small to tap.
- **The fields and tokens pages** scrolled sideways on a phone. They now show cards there.
- **The template editor's options bar** grew when something was selected, which moved the canvas under the pointer.

### Upgrading
- **No migrations,** and no settings to change.
- **Flow runs already under way** when you upgrade count their steps from 1.
- **The API** only gains a field. Every existing call keeps working as it did.
