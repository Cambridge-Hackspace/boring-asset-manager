## Search

Search now looks inside assets' fields and takes queries. It finds things by what they are, through related terms and despite typos, and it can be saved and shared. It also starts from the home page, and runs on a kiosk for signed-out visitors.

### Finding things
- **Field values.** Plain words now match the values of the fields an asset's categories give it. That includes a choice's label as well as its value, and the names a person field shows.
- **Queries.** A search with a comparison or `ORDER BY` outside quotes is a query, for example `category = Filament AND field.diameter >= 1.75 AND in ~ "shelf a" ORDER BY name`. Queries work on the asset list, the map, exports, asset sheets and stocktake checklists, in reports, and in the MCP tools. A mistake is underlined where it is. **Build a query** on the asset list writes one from lists of conditions. [Queries](https://github.com/Cambridge-Hackspace/boring-asset-manager/blob/main/docs/data-model.md#queries) has the grammar.
- **Related terms.** A new **Search terms** page in the catalog holds terms, their other names, and broader, narrower and related links. A search for "flexible filament" finds the TPU filed under it, and says why under each result. Related terms are offered as suggestions. Terms can be imported from a JSON file and removed as one import.
- **Seed from WordNet.** The Search terms page can seed terms for the nouns in your asset and category names from Princeton's WordNet 3.1. The file is downloaded once, checked against a pinned hash, and kept in the instance's storage. It can come from a mirror or be uploaded instead. Three meanings per word are taken by default, since taking every one is noisy.
- **Typos.** When nothing matches as typed or through a term, words of five letters or more may have a mistake or two. In a query, `~~` matches through related terms and despite typos, and `~` stays exact.

### Searching from anywhere
- **The home page** has a search field for anyone who may read assets. `/` goes to it from anywhere on the page. It doesn't take focus by itself, so the first Tab still reaches "Skip to content".
- **Straight to the one result.** When a search finds exactly one asset, it can open that asset's scan page instead of listing it, with a link back to all the results. This is an instance setting (`search.direct`, off by default), which each person can change under **Your account → Searching**. The asset list also offers **Scan page** beside results that have one.
- **Saved searches.** **Save as…** beside the asset list's search box keeps a search under a title. Saved searches are listed below the box and in a card on the home page. One whose query stops working, for example because a field it names was deleted, shows **needs fixing** rather than disappearing. With the new `searches.share` permission, a person can share a saved search with everyone who can read assets.
- **A kiosk search page.** A flow whose one step is the new **Search** step runs at `/f/<slug>` as a search page for a kiosk or a shop tablet. It searches the flow's categories, joined with a filter the flow sets, and shows the same results as the asset list. It clears itself after a set time without use. Marked **Open to signed-out visitors**, it searches as the public role, so it shows nothing a signed-out visitor couldn't already read. See [Flows](https://github.com/Cambridge-Hackspace/boring-asset-manager/blob/main/docs/flows.md).

### For people's own assistants
- `search_assets` describes the query language, and returns why each asset matched and the related terms it suggests.
- New MCP tools manage related terms, check and seed from WordNet, and list, run, save, change and delete saved searches.

### Upgrading
- **Back up first,** as always. This release adds three migrations: `085` (each person's choice for going straight to a result), `086` (related terms) and `087` (saved searches).
- **Two new permissions.** `terms.manage` goes to every role that manages categories. `searches.share` goes to the admin role only.
- **Searches with operators are now queries.** A search holding `=`, `<`, `>`, `~` or `ORDER BY` outside quotes is read as a query, and is refused if it isn't a valid one. Put such text in quotes to search for it as words. Scripts that pass searches to the API should check this.
- **The asset list's API** now answers a search that finds nothing with `"assets": []` rather than `null`, and adds `matched` and `suggestions` to its answer.
- **WordNet is fetched only when someone asks for it,** from `wordnetcode.princeton.edu` unless a mirror is set. Nothing is downloaded on upgrade.
