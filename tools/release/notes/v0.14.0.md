## Flows, and roles in some scopes

This release brings **flows**: guided pages for doing one everyday thing without knowing bam, such as leaving wood in the shop overnight with your name on it, then taking it home again. Roles can now also be given to someone **in some scopes only**, so one role can serve every part of a space.

### Flows
- **A flow is a guided page made of steps**, built by whoever manages flows from these kinds:
  - **Choose category:** offers the flow's categories, or always uses one.
  - **Form:** asks what the chosen category records, with your own labels, help, defaults and questions.
  - **Find:** finds something already here, by scanning its code, by search or from a list.
  - **Create:** files it, named from the answers, as `{owner}: {what}`.
  - **Label:** shows its label, queues it or prints it.
  - **Change:** archives it, sets its condition, lends or returns it, moves it, sets its fields, or presses an action.
  - **Confirm** and **Done:** say what will happen, and what did.

  Any step can be shown only when an earlier answer is something, such as only for long boards.
- **Two starters to adjust:**
  - **Leave something:** choose what it is, fill in its form, file it and label it.
  - **Collect it:** find it, confirm, archive it.
- **Made for phones and tablets.** Each flow runs at `/f/<slug>` with large controls. It works with an on-screen keyboard, a barcode scanner that types, or the camera where the browser can read codes.
- **Printable posters.** Each flow's poster has its name, a QR code that opens it, and one line of instructions. It can also be turned into a label template.
- **Done as the person running it.** A flow can only do what its runner may do, and history records each change as theirs, made through the flow: "Sam, via flow “Leave something”". Saving a flow needs every permission its steps use, so a flow never offers more than its maker could do.
- **A date to collect by.** A flow can name a date field. What it filed is then listed as past its date once that date has passed, and announced once to the notification channels that follow the new event, *Something left through a flow is past its date*.
- **Starting from the thing itself.** Scanning something a flow filed offers the flow that finishes with it, such as **Collect it**, with the thing already found. Asset pages offer it too.
- **Where to find them:** **Flows** in the menu. Those who manage flows make and edit them there, with each step beside a preview of how a phone shows it.
- **For scripts and assistants:** `/api/v1/flows`, with runs answered one step at a time, and the MCP tools `list_flows`, `flow_starters`, `save_flow`, `delete_flow` and `run_flow`.

### Roles in some scopes
- **Narrowing a role:** on the Users page, tick scopes under a role to give it in those scopes only, as "Editor, in woodshop". Ticking none gives the role as before.
- **What narrowing keeps:** only the role's permissions about assets, and only in those scopes. Everything else it grants, such as printing labels or managing users, is left out, and the page says so as you choose.
- **API tokens** can be narrowed the same way.
- **The rule against locking everyone out** counts only those who manage users in every scope.
- **API:** `PUT /api/v1/users/{id}/roles` takes `{"roles": [{"role_id", "scope_ids"}]}`, and still takes `role_ids` as before.

### Security
- **Updated dependencies:** `golang.org/x/crypto` 0.52.0, `golang.org/x/image` 0.41.0 and `filippo.io/edwards25519` 1.1.1 in the server, and `undici` 8.12.3 in the web interface's test tools.
- **None of these advisories reached bam.** The `x/crypto` ones are in its SSH packages, and bam uses only bcrypt. The `x/image` ones are in its TIFF decoder, which bam does not use. `undici` only runs in tests.

### Upgrading
- **Migrations:** `041` to `045` run when the service starts.
- **New permissions on existing roles.** Migration `043` adds two:
  - `flows.manage` to every role that manages categories;
  - `flows.run` to every role that edits assets, in the same scopes.

  So people who can edit assets can run the flows filing into their scopes. Take `flows.run` out of a role to keep its holders from running flows.
- **The built-in "Scanned" scan template** gains the quick action that offers flows, if its actions are still as they were installed. Templates you made or changed are left as they are, and the quick action only appears when there is a flow to offer.
- **Nothing else to do.** Existing role assignments, tokens and API calls keep working as they did.
