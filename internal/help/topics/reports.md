# Reports

A report finds assets with a filter, such as everything running low or everything lent out, and shows them as tables and charts. Open **Reports** to run one; it prints without the menus, and each table downloads as CSV.

Those who manage reports create and edit them. A table's columns use the same placeholders as labels; a chart groups and counts assets, or shows stock used and loans made over time.

**Scan templates** are kept here too: what scanning an asset shows. See [Scanning](/help/scanning).
