# Comments

**Comments** are notes anyone allowed can leave on an **asset**, a **category**, a **loan** or something a **flow filed**: "the left motor makes a noise above 50%", "don't lend this to first-timers", or "ordered a replacement belt".

## Where they are

- **An asset's** are on its page, under **Comments**. After its own come those on its categories, and on their parent categories, nearest first, each marked with the category it is on. Click the category's name to see the comment on the category's page. Tick **Only this asset's** to hide the rest.
- **A category's** are on its **Comments** tab, followed by its parent categories' in the same way.
- **A loan's** are in the **Loan** box on the asset's page, while it is lent: what was lent with it, or what state it went out in.
- **A filing's** are under **Filed by flows** on the asset's page, one list for each flow that filed it.

## Writing one

Comments are written in Markdown: `**bold**`, `*italics*`, `# headings`, lists, tables, `` `code` ``, links, and task lists (`- [ ] to do`, `- [x] done`). **Preview** shows how it will look. HTML you type is left out, and so is anything that could run in someone's browser.

**Attach a file** while writing to put it on the asset or category, with a link to it in the comment where your cursor is; an image shows in the comment. A loan's or a filing's files go on its asset. Images from other sites show as links, so that the site never learns who read the comment.

## Mentioning someone

Type **@** and the start of someone's name, then pick them from the list (click, or use the arrow keys and **Enter**), to mention them. Only people who can see what you are commenting on are offered. They are emailed once, with a link to the comment, unless they have turned that off under [Your account](/help/account); editing the comment emails only the people you add. A mention always shows the person's name as it is now, even if they are renamed. To people who cannot see who changed assets, it reads **@someone**.

## Editing and deleting

- You can **edit** your own comments. An edited one says *(edited)*.
- You can **delete** your own, and moderators anyone's. A deleted one leaves *Deleted by …* in its place.
- What a comment said before each edit, and before it was deleted, stays in the asset's history.

## Who can see and write them

You see the comments on whatever you can see: an asset's, its loans' and its filings' if you can see the asset, and a category's if you can see the catalog. Who wrote each shows to those who can see who changed assets.

Writing takes the **Write comments** permission, and deleting other people's the **Moderate comments** permission. Both can be given in some scopes only, for the things in those scopes. See [People and permissions](/help/people).

## Finding them

The **Assets** search also finds assets by the words in their comments, and their loans' and filings'. Notification channels can send each new comment as it is written: see [Notifications](/help/notifications).
