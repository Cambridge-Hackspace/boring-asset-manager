# Getting started

BAM! keeps track of a space's things: what there is, where it is, who has it, and how much is left.

- **Assets** are the things themselves: a drill, a bin of screws, a spool of filament. See [Assets](/help/assets).
- **Categories** group assets and give them fields to fill in, such as a serial number. See [Categories and fields](/help/categories).
- **Labels** print an asset's name and codes, to stick on it. See [Labels and printing](/help/labels).
- **Scanning** a label's code shows what the asset is and what you can do with it. See [Scanning](/help/scanning).

What you see depends on your roles: the menu at the top shows the parts you can use. On a phone they are behind **Menu**. Everyday pages sit on their own. The rest are gathered under **Catalog** (categories, fields, link types, hooks and icons), **Labels** (templates, images and printers) and **Admin** (users, roles, API tokens, notifications, backups, and import and export). Each of those opens its first page, with a row of its pages beneath the menu, and the home page lists them the same way. If something you need is missing, ask whoever manages users.

Setting BAM! up for the first time, the home page lists the steps, ticking each off as it is done: naming yourself, adding a printer, creating categories, a role for members and the people who use it, and, if you like, making some categories public and adding a notification channel.

Every page has a **Help** link at the top that opens the help for that page.

BAM! is light or dark as your device is. To choose for yourself, use **Light**, **System** or **Dark** at the bottom of any page. Signed in, the choice follows you to every device you sign in on.
