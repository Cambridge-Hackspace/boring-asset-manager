# Installing on a phone

BAM! installs on a phone, a tablet or a computer straight from this site, as an app of its own: an icon on the home screen, a window without the browser's bar, and pages that open at once. Nothing comes from an app store, and it is always the release this site runs.

## Android

Chrome offers **Install app** in its menu, and the home page shows an **Install** button when it can. Other browsers on Android have **Add to Home screen** in their menu.

## iPhone and iPad

Open the site in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. The home page says so when it is opened on an iPhone or iPad.

## Using it

Press and hold the icon for **Scan**, the **Shopping list** and **Loans**. The **Scan** page reads labels with the camera where the browser can read codes, as Chrome on Android does; on an iPhone, type the code, or open the label with the Camera app, which opens it in Safari.

The app keeps its own pages, so it opens with no signal and says it is offline rather than showing an error. What it shows, such as assets and stock, still comes from the server, so it needs a connection to open them.

## Updates

A new release is fetched in the background. **Reload to update** appears at the bottom of the window when it is ready; until then, the app stays as it is.

## Its name, icon and colors

Admins set them under **Admin › Installed app**: the name under its icon (the instance's name, unless another is given), an icon drawn from a logo, and the colors of the bar above it in light and dark. Phones that installed it pick a change up when they next check, which can take a day.
