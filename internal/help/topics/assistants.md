# Assistants

An assistant, such as Claude, can work with BAM! for you, over the Model Context Protocol (MCP): you ask in your own words, and it finds things, adds them, moves and lends them, records stock, reports problems, designs and prints labels, and runs reports. It can do anything you may do here, and nothing more.

## Connecting one

There are two ways, both on [Your account](/account) under **Assistants**:

- **Sign in.** An assistant that can sign in needs only this server's address. It sends you to a page here that says what it will be able to do; choose **Allow**, and you go back to it. It renews itself while you use it.
- **A token.** Choose **New assistant**, name it, and copy what it needs to connect, shown for Claude Code, Claude Desktop and others. The token is shown only once.

Whoever runs the server can turn assistants off for everyone; the Assistants section then says so.

## What it does as you

What it changes is recorded in each asset's history as yours, through it, such as "Ada, via Claude Desktop". It holds your permissions as they are at the time: if your roles change, so does what it may do.

It cannot change your password or make tokens, so nothing it reads can lead it to lock you out or leave itself a way back in. It can see your own assistants, and revoke them, itself included.

Before doing something that cannot be undone, such as deleting, it asks you to confirm where your assistant can show the question; otherwise your assistant's own approval asks you.

## Stopping one

Revoke it under **Assistants** on Your account, and it stops at once. Whoever manages users can revoke it too, on [API tokens](/tokens), and every assistant of yours stops when your account is turned off.

## Scripts and devices

Tokens holding roles of their own, made on [API tokens](/tokens), reach BAM! the same way, for scripts and devices rather than people. They act as themselves, not as anyone.
