# Assistants

BAM! has an assistant of its own, beside the page, once an admin turns it on. Another assistant, such as Claude, can work with BAM! for you too, over the Model Context Protocol (MCP): you ask in your own words, and it finds things, adds them, moves and lends them, records stock, reports problems, designs and prints labels, and runs reports. It can do anything you may do here, and nothing more.

## The assistant beside the page

Where an admin has turned it on, and your roles include **Use the assistant**, **Assistant** in the header opens a panel beside the page: on a phone it takes the screen, with **Back to the page**. Tell it what you have, or what you want done, in your own words, such as "there's a band saw, and it only runs when the extractor's on".

- **It sees where you are.** The asset, category, list or flow on screen goes with your message, shown above the box you type in; take it away with its **×** if it is not what you mean.
- **It does what you can.** It uses BAM!'s own tools as you, with your permissions as they are at each step, and nothing more. What it changes is recorded as yours, via the assistant.
- **You decide.** In categories that use [review states](/help/review), what it adds or changes lands at once as a draft, and the asset's page offers **Accept**, **Edit** and **Reject**. Anything else, it asks first: the panel shows what it will do, with **Confirm** and **Decline**, and nothing changes until you confirm.
- **The page keeps up.** What it changes shows on the page beside it as it happens.
- **Limits.** An instance may limit the messages and the work each person, and everyone together, can ask for each day. Reach one and the panel says when it resets.

### Guides

An admin can write guides: a purpose, such as recording the equipment in your area and what each piece needs to run, and the questions it makes sure are answered. A guide has its own address and a poster with a QR code. Open one, choose **Start**, and the panel walks you through it, showing the questions answered and those still open.

### Your conversations

Your conversations are listed on [Your account](/account), where you open or delete them. No one else can read them, admins included. They are deleted a while after their last message, 30 days unless your instance says otherwise.

What you type, the page you are on, and what the tools answer go to the model's provider, which an admin chose and was told of before turning the assistant on.

## Connecting one

There are two ways, both on [Your account](/account) under **Assistants**:

- **Sign in.** An assistant that can sign in needs only this server's address. It sends you to a page here that says what it will be able to do; choose **Allow**, and you go back to it. It renews itself while you use it.
- **A token.** Choose **New assistant**, name it, and copy what it needs to connect, shown for Claude Code, Claude Desktop and others. The token is shown only once.

Whoever runs the server can turn assistants off for everyone; the Assistants section then says so.

## What it does as you

What it changes is recorded in each asset's history as yours, through it, such as "Ada, via Claude Desktop". It holds your permissions as they are at the time: if your roles change, so does what it may do.

It cannot change your password or make tokens, so nothing it reads can lead it to lock you out or leave itself a way back in. It can see your own assistants, and revoke them, itself included.

Before doing something that cannot be undone, such as deleting, it asks you to confirm where your assistant can show the question; otherwise your assistant's own approval asks you.

## Stopping one

Revoke it under **Assistants** on Your account, and it stops at once. Whoever manages users can revoke it too, on [API tokens](/tokens), and every assistant of yours stops when your account is turned off.

## Scripts and devices

Tokens holding roles of their own, made on [API tokens](/tokens), reach BAM! the same way, for scripts and devices rather than people. They act as themselves, not as anyone.
