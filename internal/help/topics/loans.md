# Lending

Assets in a category marked **lendable** can be lent out.

- With permission to borrow, choose **Borrow** on the asset's page or its scan page, saying when it is due back if you like. Bring it back with **Return**.
- With permission to lend, you can lend to anyone, and check in any loan.
- An asset counted by weight, such as a spool, can be checked in with how much is left.

The **Loans** page lists loans, open and returned, and shows what is overdue. Borrowers' names, and who did what in an asset's history, show only to those allowed to see them; email addresses never show on asset pages.

If this BAM! sends loan reminders, set up under **Admin › Notifications**, borrowers are emailed before what they borrowed is due, the day it becomes overdue, and weekly after, with a link to return it. You can turn them off under **Email** on **Your account**.
