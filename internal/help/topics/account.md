# Your account

On **Your account** you can change the name others see, and your password. Changing your password signs you out everywhere else.

What you can do depends on your roles, listed on the same page with what each permission lets you do. A page you may not open says what it needs. If you forget your password, whoever manages users can set a new one for you. After several wrong passwords, signing in makes you wait a little before trying again, and says how long. Under **Email**, you can stop email that notification channels send to your roles from reaching you.

## Two-factor sign-in

Under **Two-factor sign-in**, you can ask for a code from an authenticator app on your phone, such as Aegis, Google Authenticator or 1Password, as well as your password. Choose **Set up an authenticator app**, scan the QR code with the app, and enter the code it shows. From then on, signing in asks for the app's code after your password, so your password alone is not enough. Each code works once.

Turning it on shows ten **recovery codes**, once. Download or print them, and keep them somewhere safe, apart from the phone. If you lose the phone, choose **Use a recovery code** when signing in and enter one: each works once. The page shows how many you have left and warns when you are running low; **Make new recovery codes** replaces them all, given a code from the app. Using one is recorded, and emails you when this BAM! sends email.

To turn it off, choose **Turn off** and give your password and a code. If you lose your phone and your recovery codes, ask whoever manages users to remove your second factor: you can then sign in with your password and set it up again.

## Assistants

An assistant, such as Claude, can work with BAM! for you over MCP: find things, add them, lend and return them, print labels. Under **Assistants** on Your account, choose **New assistant**, name it, and copy what it needs to connect: the token is shown only once. The assistant acts as you, with whatever you may do at the time, and what it changes is recorded as yours. It cannot change your password or make tokens.

Revoke an assistant there when you stop using it. [Assistants](/help/assistants) says more. Whoever manages users can revoke it too, on [API tokens](/tokens), and an assistant stops when your account is turned off.

An assistant that can sign in, such as Claude, needs no token copied: give it the server's address and it asks you to sign in to BAM! and approve it, on a page that says what it will be able to do. It is then listed under **Assistants** like any other, renewing itself while you use it, until you revoke it.
